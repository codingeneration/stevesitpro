Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about the email security audit.

How long does the audit take?

Most audits are completed within 2-3 business days. DNS changes can take up to 48 hours to fully propagate, so I schedule a follow-up check to confirm everything is working.

Will this fix my emails going to spam?

In most cases, yes. Missing or misconfigured SPF, DKIM, and DMARC records are the number one reason business emails land in spam. If there's an additional issue (like blocklist placement), I'll identify it during the audit and recommend next steps.

Do I need to give you admin access?

I'll need temporary access to your Google Admin console and your domain's DNS settings (usually through your registrar like GoDaddy, Cloudflare, or Namecheap). I'll walk you through exactly what to share. All access is revoked after the engagement.

What if I also need help with Shared Drives, security settings, or other Workspace setup?

The Starter Setup package covers email authentication plus basic Workspace cleanup and a Shared Drives blueprint. If you need more extensive work, we can scope that during the free discovery call. Check out the full Workspace setup service for details.
