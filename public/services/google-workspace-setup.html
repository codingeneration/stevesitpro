Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about the Workspace setup service.

How long does the full setup take?

For a typical small business (5-25 users), the full setup takes 2-5 business days. If you're migrating from another platform, add 1-2 days for email and data migration. We'll confirm the exact timeline during the discovery call.

Can you migrate us from Microsoft 365?

Yes. I migrate email, contacts, and calendars from Microsoft 365, legacy G Suite, IMAP, or other providers. The process is designed for zero data loss and minimal downtime — most teams experience less than 30 minutes of interruption.

We already have Workspace but it's a mess. Can you clean it up?

Absolutely. Many clients come to me with a Workspace that was set up quickly and never properly configured. The Starter Setup covers cleanup: fixing email auth, reorganizing Shared Drives, tightening security, and documenting everything.

What if we also need automation or custom workflows?

The Starter Setup focuses on infrastructure and security. For custom automation (form processing, approval workflows, notifications), check out the Automation Sprint ($1,499). Many clients do the Starter Setup first, then add automation once their foundation is solid.
